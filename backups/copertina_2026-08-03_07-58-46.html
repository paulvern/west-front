An Operational Wargame of the First World War

WESTERN FRONT

Complete manual for play, playtesting and printing

Version v1.0

An operational rulebook covering sectors, defensive lines, logistics, simultaneous orders, initiative, artillery, gas, aviation, retreats, routs, surrender, and the 1916 Verdun scenario.

Guiding principle: every action in the game must have a precise moment, a cost, a procedure, feasibility conditions, and a clear effect. If an action is not ordered when required, or not paid for when required, it cannot be carried out.

This manual is meant to be consulted at the table, not just read once before starting. Each chapter refers forward to the next using consistent terms — LP, VT, Morale, Fatigue, Operational Initiative — so that once the basic vocabulary from Part I is learned, applying it to the Verdun scenario in Part II is immediate.

Base scale: 1 turn = 1 day · 1 sector = abstract operational area · main unit = division · die = d12
