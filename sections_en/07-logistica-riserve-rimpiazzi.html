6.0 Logistics, Reserves, and Replacements

Logistics Points, abbreviated LP, represent ammunition, transport, engineers, communications, railways, trucks, horses, depots, orders, and administrative capacity.

Logistics is the engine of operations. A division may be fresh and numerous, but without LP it cannot be supported by artillery, cannot receive replacements, cannot enter from reserve, and cannot be moved effectively.

It is useful to think of LP not as an abstract currency to be spent freely, but as the concrete representation of everything needed behind the lines for an action at the front to be possible: shells must reach the batteries before they can fire, replacements must be transported and organized before reaching a division, reserves must be routed with orders and transport before entering the line. A scenario that gives a player more LP is modeling a real logistical advantage — better railways, closer depots, a more efficient administrative apparatus — not simply an arbitrary bonus.

6.1 Logistics Procedure

Procedure:
  1. Add the LP received this turn to the stored LP.
  2. Apply any scenario bonuses or penalties.
  3. Check the maximum stockpile.
  4. Pay for all actions that require LP.
  5. Record LP spent, LP remaining, and actions paid for.

6.3 Reserves

Reserves do not enter automatically. A division listed as available in the scenario moves into the Available Reserves box. To enter the map it must be paid for and ordered.

This double requirement — payment and order — reflects two distinct constraints: that of transport (the LP) and that of command (the written order, revealed together with all the others). A reserve "available" per the scenario represents a division that is ready and close to the front, but bringing it into the line still requires trains, roads, and an explicit order: it does not appear on the map simply because it is the right turn.

Step Action
1 Check that the division is available in the current turn.
2 Pay 2 LP in the Logistics Phase.
3 Write the entry order in the Orders Phase.
4 Place the division in Line 3 of a valid entry sector during the Movement Phase.
Limit: a division that has just entered cannot attack the same turn. It can defend normally.

6.5 Recruits and Experience

If a division receives even as few as 1,000 men from the Recruit pool, it immediately loses one level of Experience.

This rule forces an uncomfortable but realistic choice: quickly rebuilding a worn-down division with poorly trained recruits brings it back to full numerical strength, but dilutes its average quality, since the surviving experienced men end up a minority among inexperienced soldiers. A commander may therefore prefer to leave a division understrength but of good quality, rather than bring it back to full strength at the cost of a worse VT.
