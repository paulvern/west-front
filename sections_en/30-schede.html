Part III — Printable Sheets

Essential forms for playing and recording the state of the game.

The following sheets do not introduce any new rules: they are the practical transcription of the values and procedures defined in Part I and Part II. It is advisable to print several copies of each before starting a game: one Division Sheet for every division in play, one Sector Sheet for every sector on the map, one Logistics Sheet plus one Orders Sheet for each player every turn, and one Combat Resolution Sheet for every assault resolved. Keeping them updated turn by turn, instead of recalculating everything from memory, significantly reduces the risk of errors in applying rules such as Morale, Fatigue, or isolation, which accumulate over time and are easy to forget if not recorded.
