18.0 Victory Conditions — Isonzo 1916

Victory conditions measure control of Gorizia, the pace of the Italian advance, and the attrition inflicted on the Karst. The Italian can achieve a significant victory even without breaking through on the Karst, if he captures Gorizia within the historical timeframe; a decisive victory requires going beyond what actually happened in August 1916.

As with Verdun (chapter 16.0), the final calculation adds up territorial objectives, the pace at which they were achieved, and the attrition inflicted on the enemy, then applies any penalties for excessive losses. Section 17.13 of the scenario chronology explicitly recalls the outcome that never actually happened — Gorizia plus at least two of San Michele, Doberdò, and the Vallone del Carso — and it is exactly that outcome that defines, below, the decisive Italian victory.

18.3 Penalties for Disproportionate Losses

As at Verdun (section 16.3), these penalties apply only when losses are both high in absolute terms and lopsided relative to the opponent: heavy losses alone are not enough, there must also be a real gap against the enemy. The thresholds are lower than at Verdun, and the required gap narrower (7,000 instead of 10,000), because the scenario lasts only 8 turns and involves smaller forces: the same absolute number of losses, and the same gap between the two armies, weigh proportionally more.

Condition Penalty
The Italian has at least 25,000 losses AND Italian losses > Austro-Hungarian losses + 7,000-2 Italian VP
The Italian has at least 28,000 losses AND Italian losses > Austro-Hungarian losses + 7,000-5 Italian VP instead of -2
The Austro-Hungarian has at least 21,000 losses AND Austro-Hungarian losses > Italian losses + 7,000-2 Austro-Hungarian VP
The Austro-Hungarian has at least 25,000 losses AND Austro-Hungarian losses > Italian losses + 7,000-5 Austro-Hungarian VP instead of -2

18.5 Determining the Winner

At the end of Turn 8, add up both players' VP, apply any penalties, then calculate the difference. The same table used for Verdun (section 16.5) applies unchanged.

VP difference Result
0-3Operational draw.
4-7Marginal victory.
8-11Significant victory.
